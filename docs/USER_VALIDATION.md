# User validation — do before Nov 16 (judges explicitly reward this)

Interview 3–5 people who send money home regularly. 10 minutes each. Ask exactly:

1. What did you last send, how much, and to whom? What was it for?
2. What did it cost (fee + FX)? How long did it take?
3. Did the money pay what it was for? How do you know?
4. Has money ever been diverted / spent on something else / sent twice? What happened?
5. Would splitting it into rent / school / food envelopes help, or add hassle?
6. How would your recipient collect cash — agent nearby? Smartphone? Feature phone?

Log each interview here (first name only, date, corridor, answers in their words).
Then write one paragraph: what we changed because of what they said.
Zero interviews = say so in the pitch; three honest ones beat a claimed twenty.
